Detailed Description

Defines a fast model for the photon propagation in the photocathode thin layer model (boundary glass+photocathode, photocathode, boundary photocathode+vacuum). The photon propagation ends with photon leaving the area or absorping the photon and, eventually, producing the photoelectron leaving to the vacuum. The implementation is based on Dario Motta formalism initially, heavily rewritten. In particular, electron production depth is estimated now and electron escape probability accounts for it. Spicer three step model for electron escape was tested, but its final implementation was used only in effective way concidering experimental probability (theoretical developement did not reached good agreement with data).

Authors
